How the pipeline works

From prompt to a downloadable 30-minute film in four stages.

STEP 01

Script & storyboard

Your prompt is expanded into a full script, shot list and scene breakdown with timing.

STEP 02

Scene generation

Each scene is rendered by the long-horizon video model with locked characters and style.

STEP 03

Audio & captions

Voiceover, adaptive soundtrack and word-level subtitles are synthesized and aligned.

STEP 04

Upscale & export

Scenes are stitched, color-graded, upscaled to 4K and encoded for watermark-free download.
